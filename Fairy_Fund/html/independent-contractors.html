Overview

Filing built for people who work for themselves

When you’re self-employed, no one is withholding taxes from your pay. Your income gets reported on a Schedule C, you owe self-employment tax on top of income tax, and every legitimate business expense you can document lowers what you pay.

We sort through your 1099s and records, find the deductions that apply to your work, and file an accurate return — so you can get back to running your business.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I didn’t get a 1099. Do I still have to report the income?

Yes. Self-employment income is taxable whether or not a client sends you a 1099. If your net earnings from self-employment were $400 or more, you generally need to file a return. Bring whatever records you have — payment app history, invoices or bank deposits — and we’ll build it out from there.

What is self-employment tax?

It’s the Social Security and Medicare tax for people who work for themselves. Because no employer is splitting it with you, the rate is 15.3% on most of your net earnings. The business expenses you deduct lower the amount it’s figured on, which is why tracking them matters.

What expenses can I deduct?

Ordinary and necessary business costs — for example business mileage, supplies, the work share of your phone and internet, software, equipment, and in some cases a home office. What applies depends on your work, and we’ll go through it with you.

Do I need to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year, the IRS generally expects payments through the year instead of one lump sum in April. Due dates usually fall in April, June, September and January. We’ll help you estimate what to set aside.

How much does it cost to file?

It depends on how much is on your return — the number of 1099s, expenses and any other income. Cash clients receive exclusive discounts. Reach out and we’ll give you a quote before we start.

Can I get a refund advance?

Yes — eligible filers can receive a tax refund advance of up to $7,000 in minutes. Register early to secure your spot this season.
