Frequently Asked Questions

Your Design Questions Clearly Explained

Q1. Can you work with my existing furniture or layout?
Absolutely. We can incorporate your existing furniture, décor, and layout preferences into the new design to maintain continuity and budget efficiency.
Q2. What is the estimated cost for a design project?
Costs vary based on scope, space size, and material selections. We provide a detailed, transparent quote following your initial consultation.
Q3. Do you offer both residential and commercial design services?
Yes. Our team handles both residential and commercial projects, from private homes and apartments to offices, retail, and hospitality spaces.
Q4. Will I be involved in selecting materials and finishes?
Absolutely. Client involvement is central to our process. We present curated selections and welcome your input at every decision point.
Q5. Can you handle end-to-end execution along with design?
Yes. We offer full project management services — coordinating all contractors, trades, and deliveries so you receive a complete finished space.
